# code of conduct

## our standard

everyone participating in this project - issues, pull requests, discussions,
code review, chat - is expected to keep the interaction professional and
good-faith. concretely:

- critique code, not people. "this function leaks the key" is useful;
  "you are careless" is not
- assume competence and good intent in others; ask before assuming malice
- no harassment, personal attacks, trolling, or dogpiling
- no sexualized language or imagery, no unwelcome advances
- no publishing other people's private information without consent
- stay on topic in issue threads; take side debates elsewhere

## responsibilities

the maintainer (Jeffery Darkstar) is responsible for clarifying these
standards and for taking fair corrective action in response to behavior
this document deems unacceptable. this includes removing, editing or
rejecting comments, commits, code, issues and pull requests that violate
it, and temporarily or permanently banning contributors for behavior
the maintainer deems inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful.

## scope

these standards apply inside project spaces (issues, pull requests,
reviews, the repository itself) and whenever an individual represents
the project in public spaces.

## enforcement

report violations to the maintainer privately: github's "report user"
flow, a direct email to `ccjjfdyqlhy@outlook.com`, or a private message.
all reports are handled confidentially.

the maintainer reviews every report, gives the reported party a chance
to respond, and states the outcome to the reporter. repeated or severe
violations lead to a permanent ban. this policy may be updated at any
time; changes land as ordinary commits.
